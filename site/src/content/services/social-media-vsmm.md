VSMM is a complete small marketing department, run monthly, without hiring one. Content is planned on a calendar, produced, approved by you, published, and measured, as one repeating system rather than a scramble each week.

It runs in two tiers. Starter keeps a steady, professional presence. Growth adds more content, reels from a quarterly shoot day, and management of one ads platform. Either way, you approve every calendar before anything is posted.
