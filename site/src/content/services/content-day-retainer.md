A scheduled shoot that fills a quarter of content in one visit. Instead of scrambling for posts week by week, one planned session produces an edited photo set and a bank of video clips, ready to publish across the quarter.

It pairs naturally with social media support: the content day makes the raw material, and the monthly system plans, captions, and publishes it month by month.
