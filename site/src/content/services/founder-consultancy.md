Working sessions and written plans for founders: what to build, in what order, and just as usefully, what to skip. The output is a plan you can act on the same week, not a deck that ages on a shelf.

For ongoing direction, the consultancy continues as a founder-led retainer: a monthly strategy session, review between sessions, and a quarterly written growth plan. Implementation is scoped and quoted separately, so advice never quietly becomes a bill.
