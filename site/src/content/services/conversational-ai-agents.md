Visitors ask questions at hours your team is not working, and most of those questions are ones the business answers every day. A conversational agent answers them in your voice, books the appointment or takes the enquiry, and passes anything sensitive to a human.

The agents are built inside the same automation flows as the rest of the Automate work: connected to your booking system and your CRM, tested against your real questions, and launched with clear limits on what they may say and do.
